Gas Stove
  1. Press the gas Power button first. The remaining time should appear on the screen before using the stove.
  2. Pull out the hood panel to turn the hood on.
  3. Push the stove knob in, then turn it counterclockwise to the ignition position until the flame lights.
  4. If the remaining time flashes, press ▲ UP to add more time. The gas may shut off automatically after a short while.
  5. After cooking, turn all stove knobs to OFF, push the hood panel back in, and press the gas Power button to turn it off.
‼️

Important: The timer does not turn the stove knobs off. Always make sure all stove knobs are OFF after cooking.

Washing Machine
👕

For everyday laundry, please use the Standard Wash cycle.

  • Add detergent to the left compartment only.
  • Use only 1 cup, about 30 ml of detergent.
  • Turn the dial to 표준세탁 (Standard Wash), then press Start/Pause.
  • When the wash is complete, please move the laundry to the dryer.
Dryer
👕

For everyday laundry, please use the Standard cycle.

  • Place the wet laundry inside and add 1 dryer sheet.
  • Press Power, select Standard, then press Start/Pause.
  • When the cycle is complete, please remove the laundry.
  • After every use, please remove lint from the filter.
  • Use small loads only. Standard drying capacity is 2.5 kg.
Air Conditioner
🌡️

The recommended temperature is 24-26°C.

✔️

The air conditioner remote control is located under the TV, together with the TV remote.

The air conditioner is already set to Cooling mode. Please use only the buttons below.

  • Press the blue Power button to turn the air conditioner on or off.
  • Press ▲ / ▼ to adjust the temperature.
  • Press 풍향 (Air Direction) to change the airflow direction.
  • Please turn off the air conditioner when you go out to help protect the environment.
Heating Controls
⚠️

Do not use the heating and air conditioner at the same time. This may cause power overload, system problems, or fire risk.

🌡️

Keep the temperature between 20°C and 24°C. Floor heating may take 1-3 hours to warm up.

  • Press the Power button to turn the heating on or off.
  • Use ▲ / ▼ to adjust the temperature.
  • Use Hot Water Only mode when you only need hot water.
  • Use Away mode when leaving the room. It helps save energy.
  • To prevent malfunction, the host may adjust the heating system remotely via IoT if needed.
TV
📺

The TV remote control is located under the TV.

  1. Press the red Power button at the top left of the remote control to turn on the TV.
  2. The TV may take a little while to turn on. Please wait for a moment.
  3. To watch Netflix or YouTube, press the Netflix or YouTube button near the bottom of the remote after the TV turns on.

03 | WASTE & CLEANLINESS
Toilet Clog Guide

If the toilet is blocked, please use the Mr. Pung toilet plunger as shown below.

  1. Insert the CO₂ cartridge into the opening as shown.
  2. Close the lid until it is secure.
  3. Place the plunger head over the toilet drain and press down firmly.
  4. Press the round button on top once.
⚠️

Cautions: Use it only when there is enough water in the toilet bowl. Push the plunger head all the way to the drain, and insert the CO₂ cartridge right before use.

❗

This tool uses strong CO₂ pressure to clear the blockage. Please try the steps above once. If it does not work, please contact the host.

Indoor Food Waste & Recycling Guide
🍎

Food Waste = Use the food-waste bin only

Please put all food waste in the food-waste bin on the kitchen sink counter.

🗑️

General Waste & Recyclables = Separate in the living room bins

Please place general waste and recyclables in their separate bins in the living room.

  • Extra trash bags are in the bottom drawer of the sink cabinet.
  • If a bin becomes full or odors are a concern, please follow Outdoor Waste Disposal & Recycling below.

Food Waste

  • Please collect food waste in the food-waste bin on the kitchen sink counter.
  • Close the lid tightly after use.
  • To prevent odors, please take it outside at least once every 2 days by following Outdoor Waste Disposal & Recycling below.

General Waste

General waste means non-recyclable waste, excluding food waste and recyclables.

  • The general waste bin already has a trash bag inside. Please put general waste only in this bin.
  • Do not mix recyclables with general waste.
  • If the bin becomes full, please tie the bag and take it outside.

Recyclables

  • Boxes and paper bags: flatten them and place them together in one area.
  • Please lightly rinse food delivery containers before separating them.
Outdoor Waste Disposal & Recycling
🚪

Please place waste at the marked spot right outside the building entrance, along the wall.

Disposal Location

  • Recyclables: Place them at the marked recyclables spot.
  • General waste: Tie the bag and leave it at the marked general waste spot.
  • Food waste: Put the tied bag inside the green food-waste bin.

Food Waste

  1. Tie the food-waste bag.
  2. Open the green bin.
  3. Put the bag inside.
  4. Close the lid.

General Waste / Recyclables

Take these out only if needed.

  • General waste: Tie the bag and leave it at the marked spot.
  • Boxes / paper bags: Leave them together.
  • Vinyl / plastic: Put these out first.
  • Cans / paper / other recyclables: Separate each type.
  • Glass, cans, paper, etc. should be placed in separate bags.
